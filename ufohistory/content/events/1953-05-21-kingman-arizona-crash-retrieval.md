On the morning of **20 May 1953**, Arthur Stansel Jr. — an engineer with the Air Research and Development Command at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base — wrote in his diary: *"funny call from Dr. D about 1000. I'm going on a special job tomorrow."*

The following day, Stansel and a group of other specialists were transported by a blacked-out bus for approximately three to four hours to a remote desert location in the Kingman, Arizona area. On arrival they were escorted individually to view a craft that had come down in the desert.

## The Craft and Crew

Stansel described the object as roughly **30 feet in diameter**, oval in shape, with a **brushed-aluminium appearance** — undamaged, as though it had landed rather than crashed hard. Nearby, inside a tent, were **two bodies**: small figures with **brown leathery skin** and **silver skull caps**.

## Personnel

**Dr. Ed Doll** — Dr. Edward Bushnell Doll, at the time a senior figure at TRW's predecessor organisation — is named by Stansel as the coordinator who summoned him to the operation ("Dr. D" in the diary entry). Doll had a background in military effects research, having directed the military effects group during Operation Upshot-Knothole in March 1953, just weeks before the Kingman event.

**Dr. Eric Henry Wang** is named as the lead of the reverse engineering team assigned to the craft.

## Disclosure

Stansel did not disclose the event publicly until **1973**, when he was interviewed by UFO researcher **Raymond Fowler**. He spoke under the pseudonym **"Fritz Warner"** and subsequently signed a sworn affidavit attesting to his account. The affidavit and Fowler's notes form the primary documentary record of the incident.

## Significance

The Kingman retrieval sits within the same operational window as a cluster of alleged crash retrievals in the early 1950s. It is notable for the specificity of the witness account, the named personnel, and the diary corroboration predating the disclosure by twenty years. The involvement of Doll — who would go on to become Executive Vice President of TRW Systems Group — and Wang points to a network of engineers connected to both government weapons programmes and alleged non-human technology recovery.
