In **October 2017**, musician and UAP researcher **Tom DeLonge** publicly launched **To The Stars Academy of Arts & Science (TTSA)** — a public benefit corporation structured around three operational divisions: Entertainment, Science, and Aerospace. The organisation's stated goal was to investigate the UAP phenomenon using resources and personnel drawn from the highest levels of the US government and defence community, and to pressure for greater official transparency.

## Key Events Leading to the Launch

**October 4, 2017:** **Luis Elizondo**, who had run the Pentagon's classified [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/events/2012-aatip-closed) since at least 2009, resigned from the Department of Defense citing institutional resistance to the issue. In his resignation letter to Defence Secretary James Mattis he wrote: *"Why aren't we spending more time and effort on this issue?"* He joined TTSA immediately upon resigning.

**September 2017:** In advance of the public launch, TTSA had already opened a **$50 million public equity crowdfunding campaign** (Regulation A+), inviting members of the public to become shareholders — an unusual step for a company of this nature, and one that generated immediate scrutiny.

## The December 2017 NYT Release
Within weeks of the launch, TTSA co-ordinated with *The New York Times*, *The Washington Post*, and *Politico* to publish simultaneous bombshell stories on **December 16, 2017**, revealing AATIP and releasing three declassified UAP videos. See: [New York Times Publishes UAP Article (December 2017)](/events/2017-new-york-times-publishes-uap-article).

## Later Developments
- 2018: SEC filings reveal a $37.4 million deficit; DeLonge disputes the characterisation.
- 2019: TTSA enters a Co-operative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA) with the US Army. History Channel airs *Unidentified: Inside America's UFO Investigation*.
- April 2020: The Pentagon officially releases the three UAP videos TTSA had published in 2017–18.
- Late 2020: Elizondo departs TTSA.
- 2022: TTSA rebrands as **To The Stars**; DeLonge rejoins Blink-182.

## Significance
TTSA represented an unprecedented convergence of entertainment, science, and former-intelligence-community figures focused on UAP disclosure. Its co-ordination of the 2017 media releases is widely credited with transforming UAP from a fringe subject into a legitimate national security concern, directly contributing to the creation of the UAP Task Force, AARO, and the Congressional hearings of 2022–2023.
