## Key Events and Claims

### Early Years
- **1975:** Thomas Matthew DeLonge born December 13.
- **Mid-1990s:** Co-founds Blink-182. While on tour, reads Timothy Good's *Above Top Secret* — a detailed chronology of UFO events alongside military and space programmes — which he later credits as the starting point of his obsession.
- **1999:** Blink-182's album *Enema of the State* includes "Aliens Exist," which references the Majestic 12 group.

### Quietly Building Contacts
- Throughout the early 2000s, DeLonge studies declassified documents and UFO whistleblower accounts, and accumulates ~136 hours of video testimonies from government employees who claim knowledge of UAP activity.
- Begins making contact with intelligence and military insiders over roughly a decade.

### 2015: Leaving Blink-182 and To The Stars
- In 2015 DeLonge departs Blink-182 to focus on UAP disclosure.
- Founds **To The Stars, Inc.** as an entertainment company.

### 2017: To The Stars Academy of Arts & Science (TTSA)
- **September 2017:** TTSA launches a $50 million public equity crowdfunding campaign (Regulation A+).
- **October 4, 2017:** Luis Elizondo resigns from the DoD's AATIP, citing frustration at internal resistance. He joins TTSA.
- **October 2017:** TTSA officially launches publicly. Founding team includes:
  - **Tom DeLonge** (founder)
  - **Luis Elizondo** (former AATIP director)
  - **Hal Puthoff** (former NSA researcher, Institute for Advanced Studies at Austin)
  - **Steve Justice** (ret. Director, Advanced Systems, Lockheed Skunkworks)
  - **Jim Semivan** (25 years, CIA Directorate of Operations)
  - **Christopher Mellon** (former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence)

### December 2017: The New York Times Bombshell
- **December 16, 2017:** The *New York Times*, *Washington Post*, and *Politico* simultaneously publish stories revealing the existence of AATIP and releasing three declassified UAP videos:
  - **FLIR1** ("Tic Tac") — USS Nimitz, November 2004
  - **GIMBAL** — USS Theodore Roosevelt, January 2015
  - **GOFAST** — USS Theodore Roosevelt, 2015
- TTSA co-ordinated the release with the Times (reporters Leslie Kean, Ralph Blumenthal, and Helene Cooper).

### 2018: Financial Scrutiny
- An Ars Technica report reveals TTSA had accumulated a **$37.4 million deficit** per a mid-year SEC filing. DeLonge disputes the characterisation.

### 2019: Government Partnership and Television
- TTSA enters a Co-operative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA) with the US Army to share research into "metamaterials" and other advanced technologies.
- History Channel airs *Unidentified: Inside America's UFO Investigation* (6-part series), hosted by Elizondo and Mellon.

### April 2020: Pentagon Officially Releases Videos
- The US Navy officially declassifies and releases the three UAP videos (FLIR1, GIMBAL, GOFAST) originally published by TTSA in 2017–2018.

### 2021–2022: Congressional Impact and Reunion
- TTSA's lobbying work — including helping draft whistleblower protection language in the National Defense Authorization Act — contributes to the creation of the UAP Task Force and later AARO.
- In 2022 DeLonge rejoins Blink-182 for a reunion tour; Mark Hoppus declares "Tom was right" before playing "Aliens Exist."

### 2023: Congressional Hearings
- Whistleblower **David Grusch** cites TTSA's groundwork in his 2023 Congressional testimony on UAP crash retrieval programmes.
- DeLonge's advocacy is credited with helping normalise UAP as a legitimate national security subject.

### 2022 Rebrand
- To The Stars Academy formally rebrands as **To The Stars**.
- Elizondo had departed the company in late 2020.
