## Key Claims and Research Areas

### Northrop Grumman's Alleged Role
- Described as one of the premier US defense contractors alleged to possess non-human vehicles and technologies of unknown origin (TUO).
- Alleged to operate TUO material exploitation teams and UFO crash retrieval units alongside Lockheed Martin and SAIC.
- The Northrop Advanced Technology & Design Center (the company's equivalent of Lockheed's Skunkworks) disappeared from public record in the late 1990s — its fate is a focus of the investigation.

### TRW and the 2002 Acquisition
- Northrop Grumman acquired TRW Inc. (Thompson Ramo Wooldridge) on **1 July 2002** for **$7.8 billion** (initial hostile bid: $5.9 billion in February 2002; Northrop outbid BAE Systems and General Dynamics).
- TRW was heavily involved in satellite, missile, and advanced space systems including Pioneer 10/11, the Defense Support Programme (DSP), Tracking and Data Relay Satellite System, and the first US ICBM.
- TRW spawned the **Aerospace Corporation** (an FFRDC) in 1960, which continues as an independent non-profit advising the US government on space systems.
- In 2003 Northrop paid **$111 million** to settle a lawsuit against TRW for "overcharging of space programmes" — a detail UAP Gerb links to the **Wilson-Davis memo** (Eric Davis / Adm. Thomas Wilson, alleged 2002 meeting).

### The Aerospace Corporation / FFRDC Structure
- UAP Gerb's framework holds that within UAP legacy programmes, defense prime contractors operate at the lowest knowledge/access threshold, typically through FFRDCs and University Affiliated Research Centers (UARCs) under government handlers.
- The institutional chain described: Major Range and Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs — Edwards, Dugway, Tonopah) → FFRDCs (Sandia, Battelle, Aerospace Corporation) → prime contractors (Northrop, Lockheed, SAIC).

### Stephanie O'Sullivan and ODNI
- Whistleblower **David Grusch** (in media interviews from 2025–2026, including the Megyn Kelly interview) alleged that **Stephanie O'Sullivan** — former Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence (PDDNI) and former TRW engineer, now chair of the Aerospace Corporation board — managed the UAP crash retrieval issue alongside **James Clapper** at ODNI.
- The TRW → Northrop → Aerospace Corporation personnel lineage is therefore directly implicated in whistleblower allegations.

### Independent Research and Development (IRAD)
- UAP Gerb alleges that Northrop leveraged IRAD funds — contractor-funded R&D expenses reimbursed by the DoD — to self-finance UAP exploitation programmes after 9/11 ("War on Terror" era).
- A 1982 CIA memo is cited in which the Agency expressed interest in funnelling IRAD funds through select contractors including TRW.
- TRW was implicated in over half a dozen IRAD fraud schemes prior to the Northrop acquisition.

### Project ZODIAC
- TRW is named as a prime contractor in an alleged classified programme called **ZODIAC**, details of which remain obscure.

### Key Acquisitions
