Title: Terry W. Phillips
Description: Former Executive Director of the AFOSI Office of Special Projects (PJ) and SES-1 Air Force SAP Security Director; subsequently SVP and CSO at Leidos and VP Security at Northrop Grumman; named by UAP researcher UAP Gerb and whistleblower Matthew Brown as a key figure in the legacy programme security apparatus.

**Terry W. Phillips** is a former senior Air Force counterintelligence and special access programme (SAP) security official whose post-government career has taken him through Leidos and then to Northrop Grumman — placing him, in UAP research terms, at the nexus of the alleged legacy programme security structure.

## Career

**Education**
- B.S. Mathematics, Auburn University (1991)
- M.A. Education Administration, Chapman University (1994)
- Air Force Special Investigations Academy (1997)
- M.S. National Resource Strategy, National Defense University (2016)

**Air Force career (1992–2022)**

*Early career:*
- 1992–1994: Executive Officer, 330th Flying Training Squadron, Castle AFB, CA
- 1994–1996: Emergency Actions Officer, 86th Airlift Wing, Ramstein AB, Germany
- 1997–1998: Special Agent, AFOSI, Pope AFB, NC (first OSI assignment following the Special Investigations Academy)

*Counterintelligence and SAP security:*
- 2001–2004: Director of Operations, OSI Anti-Terrorism Specialty Team, Lackland AFB, TX; deployed 2003–04 to Kirkuk AB, Iraq (Bronze Star Medal)
- 2004–2006: Assistant Director for Plans and Readiness, HQ AFOSI
- 2006–2009: Deputy Director, OSI Field Investigations Region 3, Scott AFB, IL
- 2009–2012: Director of Counterintelligence, US European Command (also US Counterintelligence Representative to NATO)
- 2012–2015: Director of Counterintelligence, HQ AFOSI, Quantico, VA
- 2016: Deputy Director, AFOSI Office of Special Projects (PJ)
- January 2017 – 2022: **Executive Director, AFOSI Office of Special Projects (PJ)**, and concurrently **USAF/Space Force Special Access Program (SAP) Security Director**

**AFOSI PJ (Office of Special Projects)**
The PJ office within AFOSI is the Air Force component responsible for protecting Special Access Programmes — black programmes — from foreign intelligence penetration and insider threats. As Executive Director, Phillips directed **427 personnel across 41 field locations** and oversaw protection of **$30 billion in annual obligations** supporting classified research, development, testing, and production of SAPs across the US Air Force, Space Force, and select DoD SAPs.

On **6 August 2020**, Phillips was promoted to **SES-1** (Senior Executive Service Level 1) — the first Executive Director of the PJ office to achieve this rank.

**Leidos (January 2022)**
Appointed **Senior Vice President and Chief Security Officer (CSO)** at Leidos on 6 January 2022. Led the Global Security Organisation for a major US defence contractor with extensive intelligence community contracts.

**Northrop Grumman (2023–present)**
Currently **Vice President, Security** at Northrop Grumman. The exact transition date from Leidos to Northrop is not in the public record, but occurred sometime between 2022 and 2023.

## UAP Allegations

Phillips is not named in any UAP congressional testimony or official UAP document in the public record.

His UAP-relevant connection derives from:
- **UAP Gerb's investigative analysis**, which identifies him as part of the security architecture overseeing alleged legacy programmes at Northrop Grumman.
- A statement attributed to whistleblower **Matthew Brown**, who identified Phillips as "one of the key players in the Legacy security architecture" and "the current tip of the spear" at Northrop in the context of alleged UAP programme security.

His career trajectory — AFOSI counterintelligence → PJ (SAP protection) → Leidos → Northrop Grumman VP Security — follows the pattern of senior government SAP security officials moving into positions at precisely the contractors alleged to hold legacy programme materials.
